# HD-2026-001
## Date Completed: August 4, 2026
## Priority: low
## Category: Account update

### Problem
A user got married and needs to get her legal name changed. I need to change the display name, email address, but also keep the old address as an alias for the user. 

### Troubleshooting Process: 
1.	Navigated to the company Directory containing all user accounts
2.	Located the user’s account
3.	Opened the account’s edit settings
4.	Updated the user’s legal/display name
5.	Updated the primary email address to reflect the user’s new name
6.	Retained the user’s previous email as an email alias so messages sent to the former address would continue to reach the user.

### Resolution: 
The user’s display name and primary email address were successfully updated, and the previous email address was retained as an alias.

Resolved
