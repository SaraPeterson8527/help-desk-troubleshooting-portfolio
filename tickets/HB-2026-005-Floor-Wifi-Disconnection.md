# HB-2026-005
## Date Completed: September 22, 2026
## Priority:  Critical
## Category: Wifi disconnection on Floor3

### Problem: 
3rd floor internet was not working properly for about half an hour. Customer checked other floors and they were working fine. Conference rooms were out as well making multiple departments unable to work and customer service not accessing CRM.

### Troubleshooting Process: 
1. Checked the server rooms to see if any of the devices were out.
2. It seemed like 7/8 devices were online but 1 was down.
3. Investigated the down device and it turned out to be Floor 3 switch.
4. I clicked that switch and rebooted it and it went back online quickly.
5. Checked with customer and make sure connection was established again and it was.

### Resolution: 
Floor 3 wifi wasn't working properly while other floors' wifi were working perfectly fine. Checked the server rooms and found the problem quickly and made sure the connection was established again.

Resolved
